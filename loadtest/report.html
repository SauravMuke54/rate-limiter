What to look for

  • 5xx Errors should be 0 — anything above zero means an unhandled exception surfaced under load rather than a clean error response.
  • p95/p99 latency should stay well below your SLA even as VUs ramp up in the ramp_up scenario — a sudden knee indicates the proxy's saturation point.
  • 200 + 429 should account for ~all requests — a large "Unexpected Status" count suggests something is returning codes outside the expected 200/429/502/504 range.
